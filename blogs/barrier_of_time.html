[ Within the Barrier of Time ] [ 在时间结界之内 ]

Maybe The Three-Body Problem has left too deep a mark on me.

I find the whole setup of countries, nationalities, and borders unbearably boring.

I truly believe they will eventually be abolished, and that we are the sacrificed generation.

They are so boring that I might as well call them QWE.

When QWE is finally erased, who knows how far human technology will have enabled us to travel through space.
Perhaps interstellar travel will already be possible by then.

But for humans born into this age like us and still bound by QWE, Earth is the only map we are allowed to walk.

We look up at the sky, yet our feet remain on the ground, because between us and everything beyond Earth lies a vast barrier of time.

How hopeless that is. And yet, there is nothing we can do about it now.

BUT ON TOP OF THIS POWERLESSNESS, WHAT HAVE HUMANS DONE?

They have built yet more barriers on Earth itself.

How absurd.

也许是受《三体》影响太深了。

我觉得国家、国籍、国界的设置太无聊了。

我坚信他们终将被取缔，而我们则是被牺牲一代。

太无聊了所以统称他们为 QWE 吧。

当 QWE 被消灭的时候，不知道那时候人类科技能助力人类在什么范围的空间移动。
也许已经实现星际旅行了也说不定。

但对我们这些被 QWE 束缚的人类来说，地球是我们唯一可以行走的版图。

我们仰望天空但只能脚踏实地。因为我们与地球之外隔着漫长的时间结界。

这是多么令人绝望的事，但现在的我们无可奈何。

但是在这无奈之上，现在的人类干了什么？

他们在地球上又人造了结界！

多么荒唐！
